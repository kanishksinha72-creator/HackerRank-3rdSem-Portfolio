## Problem: Compare the Triplets

**Link:** [HackerRank - Compare the Triplets](https://www.hackerrank.com/challenges/compare-the-triplets/problem)

### Approach

I compare Alice's and Bob's ratings at each of the three positions. If Alice's rating is higher, her score is increased. If Bob's rating is higher, his score is increased. Equal ratings do not give either person a point.

### Complexity

- **Time:** O(1)
- **Space:** O(1)

### Notes

The three ratings are compared element by element, and the final scores are returned as a two-element array.
